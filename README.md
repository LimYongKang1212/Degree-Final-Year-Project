<h2 align="left">Introduction 📝📝📝 </h2>

###

<p align="justify">Sentiment analysis is a method for identifying the emotions or subjective information contained in a piece of text from a variety of sources, such as daily posts and comments on social media by university students. University students often use social media as a forum to freely share and comment on their real ideas and experiences in university life.</p>

###

<h2 align="left">Problem Statement 🤔🤔🤔 </h2>

###

<p align="justify">• University Students daily use social media to share their true feelings without thinking about any limitations or obstacles.<br><br>• This situation make it difficult for universities to collect information, and it is time-consuming to analyxe these sentences.</p>

###

<h2 align="left">Objective 🎯🎯🎯</h2>

###

<p align="justify">1. To determine the most sentences of a positive and negative sentiment of the key or particular words about UUM students' opinion<br><br>2. To identify key or particular words about UUM students' opinions on social media<br><br>3. To develop a sentiment analysis monitoring tool to analyze and monitor social media content.<br><br>4. To classify and validate the positive and negative sentiment of the key or particular words about students' opinions</p>

###

<h2 align="left">Methodology 🛠️🛠️🛠️</h2>

###

<h2 align="left">Analysis 📊📊📊 </h2>

###

<p align="justify">• USE questionnaire is employed to get feedback from the management authorities of each department at UUM and validate the model's performance</p>

###

<p align="justify">• Evaluation was conducted with 20 management officials and employees from the Chancellery Department, Perpustakaan Sultanah Bahiyah, and School of Computing at the University Utara Malaysia (UUM) by a stratified sampling technique</p>

###

<h2 align="left">Results 📋📋📋 </h2>

###

<p align="justify">The result of the evaluation was positive feedback from the respondent. The F1 scores for both sentiments indicate that positive sentiments are detected with 100% accuracy and negative sentiments with 94.83% accuracy.</p>
