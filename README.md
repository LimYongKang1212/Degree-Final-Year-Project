<h1 align="left">Degree Final Year Project - Student Sentiment Analysis In Social Media: A Monitoring Tool for Student Satisfaction</h1>

###

<h2 align="left">Introduction</h2>

###

<br clear="both">

<p align="left">Sentiment analysis is a method for identifying the emotions or subjective information contained in a piece of text from a variety of sources, such as daily posts and comments on social media by university students. University students often use social media as a forum for freely sharing or making comments about their real ideas and experiences in university life.</p>

###

<h2 align="left">Problem Statement</h2>

###

<p align="left">• University Students daily use social media to share their true feelings without thinking about any limitations or obstacles.<br><br>• This situation make it difficult for universities to collect information, and it is time-consuming to analyxe these sentences.</p>

###

<h2 align="left">Objective</h2>

###

<p align="left">1. To determine the most sentences of a positive and negative sentiment of the key or particular words about UUM student's opinion<br><br>2. To identifykey or particular words about UUM students' opinions on social media<br><br>3. To develop a sentiment analysis monitoring tool to analyze and monitor social media content.<br><br>4. To classify and validate the positive and negative sentiment of the key or particular words about students' opinions</p>

###

<h2 align="left">Methodology</h2>

###

<p align="left">• Waterfall Development had been employed to develop the "UUM Student Satisfaction Analysis Monitoring Tool"</p>

###

<p align="left">• The sentiment analysis model, titled the "UUM Student Satisfaction Analysis Monitoring Tool," was designed based on the requirements, and the evaluation of the effectiveness of the model is done by field testing.</p>

###

<h2 align="left">Analysis</h2>

###

<p align="left">• USE questionnaire is employed to get feedback from the management authorities of each department at UUM and validate the model's performance</p>

###

<p align="left">• Evaluation was conducted with 20 management officials and employees from the Chancellery Department, Perpustakaan Sultanah Bahiyah, and School of Computing at the University Utara Malaysia (UUM) by stratified sampling technique</p>

###

<h2 align="left">Results</h2>

###

<p align="left">The result of the evaluation was positive feedback from the respondent. The result of F1 score for both sentiments is positive sentiment are displaying 100% accurately detected and negative sentiment are detected 94.83% accurately.</p>

###

<h2 align="left">Conclusion</h2>

###

<p align="left">• It was proven that the UUM Student Satisfaction Analysis Monitoring Tool is valuable for users to analyse the student’s opinion on social media to solve real-life problems and improve the management of the university.<br><br>• Some further improvements are needed, such as enhancing the sentiment analysis dictionary, incorporating clear topic detection, and enabling automatic analysis by file, to make the monitoring tool even more useful and effective.</p>
